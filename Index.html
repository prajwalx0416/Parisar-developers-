Documents to check before you buy

Ask for these before buying any plot. We share the documents on request.

  • RERA registration certificate
  • GUDA-approved layout plan
  • Land conversion order
  • RTC (Pahani) extract
  • Encumbrance certificate (EC)
  • Sale deed and earlier title documents
